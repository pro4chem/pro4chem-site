Paradigm Shift in Polymer Technology

LuXyl exterior-resistance and mirror-finish hybrid resin family is positioned around Migration to Hybrid Technology: multifunctional hybrid polymer technology that helps make better finished products, easier formulation, easier use, and equal or lower final formulation cost.

Product detail - LuXyl exterior-resistance and mirror-finish hybrid resin family

Known product detail for review: LX-3300, LX-3301, LX-3307, LZ-6510 where waterborne mirror-finish positioning applies. Target applications: Exterior-resistant industrial maintenance, commercial fleet refinish, premium gloss, and high-durability systems. Hybrid advantage: LumaCore and Impenetra technology for finish brilliance and exterior durability positioning. Formulation benefit: Maximum Exterior Resistance. Mirror Finish Brilliance. Evaluation lens: finished formulation cost and total cost of application.

Sales advantage review

Buyer advantage: Specialty industrial and refinish lines seeking exterior resistance and high DOI/mirror finish. Finished-product value: Better finished products with gloss retention direction, exterior durability, chemical resilience, and premium appearance. Cost message: Use total cost of application to compare durability and maintenance value, not only resin price.
