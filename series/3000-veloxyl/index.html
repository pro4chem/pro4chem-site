Performance story

FerraXyl FX-3127 leads the platform as a next-generation self-crosslinking hybrid resin combining fast cure, molecular corrosion protection, brilliant durable finishes, and strong 1K/2K cost-performance.

FerraXyl FX-3127 platform value

FerraXyl excels in premium 1K and 2K coatings with fast or very fast curing, lower resin dosage, simplified formulation, and brilliant highly durable finishes.

1K / 2K flexibility

Use it as a practical self-curing 1K finish or crosslink it with isocyanate for extreme-resistance 2K polyurethane performance.
