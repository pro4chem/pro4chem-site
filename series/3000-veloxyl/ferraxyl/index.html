FerraXyl anticorrosive hybrid resin family

FerraXyl is the Series 3000 solvent-borne hybrid resin family for anticorrosive DTM, primer, and premium 1K/2K coatings. FX-3127 leads the family with fast or very fast curing, molecular corrosion protection, simplified formulation, lower resin dosage framing, and brilliant durable gloss.

Performance story

FerraXyl is the Series 3000 solvent-borne hybrid resin family for anticorrosive DTM, primer, and premium 1K/2K coatings. FX-3127 leads the family with fast or very fast curing, molecular corrosion protection, simplified formulation, lower resin dosage framing, and brilliant durable gloss.

Performance

Self-crosslinking hybrid polymer architecture supports practical 1K curing and 2K polyurethane crosslinking routes with fast production rhythm, hard film development, and brilliant high-gloss appearance.

Applications

Designed for anticorrosive DTM systems, primers, protective topcoats, industrial maintenance finishes, metal coatings, and premium high-resistance 1K/2K coating projects.

Differentiation

CorroGuard molecular shielding, Xdry cure acceleration, and LumaCore finish direction help replace multi-product anticorrosive stacks with one focused hybrid resin route.

Implementation outcomes

Formulators can evaluate lower resin dosage, simpler recipes, stronger cost-performance, and faster handling without losing the premium finish quality expected from modern protective coatings.
